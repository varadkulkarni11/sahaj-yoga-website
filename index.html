How it works

Three sessions in, you should feel the difference.

Nothing here is a group routine handed to everybody. The sequence you get is built for your body, and it changes as your body changes.

  1. 1

    Tell us the problem

    A message or a call. What hurts, since when, what your doctor has said, what you have already tried. Bring reports if you have them.

  2. 2

    We watch you move

    A first session to see your posture, your range, and what triggers the pain. This is where the real assessment happens.

  3. 3

    You get your own practice

    Asanas chosen for your condition, held with ropes and belts where support is needed, plus pranayama. Adjusted every week as you improve.

What we teach

Six ways to practise with us.

Rope and belt therapy

One to one, in person

Wall ropes and belts take the load off a painful joint so you can hold a posture correctly and for longer. This is the core of how we treat knee, back and neck pain, and how we work with stiff or injured bodies.

Medical yoga therapy

One to one, in person or online

For a specific complaint: back, neck, shoulder, knee, sciatica, cervical spondylosis, recovery after surgery. Short focused courses, usually a few weeks, with a home practice you continue on your own.

Weight loss

Group or one to one

Active sequences built around what your joints can take, with breathing work and steady lifestyle changes rather than crash routines.

PCOD and PCOS

One to one or small group

Asanas and pranayama for hormonal balance and cycle regularity, alongside whatever your gynaecologist has advised.

Daily group classes

In person and online, with recordings

Warm-up, asanas and pranayama in a balanced sequence. Students join from Pune, the United States and Singapore. Recordings are shared, so a different time zone or a missed morning is not a problem.

Prenatal yoga

One to one or small group

Safe movement, breathing and rest through each stage of pregnancy, modified as it progresses.
